# Client Brief: Mehr Bakery (fictional)

Mehr Bakery is a family bakery in Lahore. Customers find them on a phone, but the current page is hard to read on a small screen and gives no clear way to ask about an order.

## Visitor

The typical visitor is planning a birthday or family event, on her phone. She wants to see what the bakery makes and ask one question.

## Page structure

1. **First screen:** she must see who Mehr Bakery is and what it sells.
2. **Below that:** a short menu, readable with one thumb.
3. **Then:** an enquiry form with name, phone and message.

## Feel

Warm and handmade, not corporate.

- Cream background
- Deep brown text
- One warm accent colour
- A serif font for headings
- Generous spacing
- CSS-only decoration, because there are no photos yet

## Content rules

- Every menu item, price and review is SAMPLE and must carry a visible SAMPLE label.
- The phone number is the placeholder +92 300 0000000.
- No invented address, opening hours or awards.

## Enquiry form: TEST MODE only

- On send, save the enquiry in the browser and list it on a separate page, `inbox.html`, labelled TEST INBOX.
- Send nothing over the internet.

## Build

- One page, mobile first
- Plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript
- No frameworks, no build step
- Must open with a double click
