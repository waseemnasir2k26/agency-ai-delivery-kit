# Brand voice (fictional clients, demo only)

- **Mehr Bakery:** warm, short sentences, family bakery since forever. Prices always as `PKR 1234`. Never "cheap", say "everyday".
- **Sufi Threads:** calm, crafted, seasonal. Never shout sales; say "the winter edit".
- **Noor Dental Clinic:** clear, reassuring, no medical claims, no promises of results. Bookings via the front desk only.
