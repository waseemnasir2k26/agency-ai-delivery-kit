# Proposal: Sufi Threads winter page

## Scope
- One new winter collection page on the existing site, in the current style.
- Sections for khaddar suits, embroidered shawls and velvet kurtas.
- Winter prices shown for each item.
- A launch offer for the first week, only if you confirm one and its terms. If there is none, the page shows none.
- A fitting booking form on the page, with enquiries sent to the client.

## Unknowns
The build starts only when every one of these is answered:
- Winter price of the khaddar suit, embroidered shawl and velvet kurta.
- Whether there is a launch offer, its terms, and the launch week dates.
- Days and times available for fittings.
- What the booking form should ask for.
- Where fitting enquiries are sent.
- The date the page must be live.
- Who approves the page.
- Which photos and item descriptions will be supplied.

## Client provides
- Answers to every question in `unknowns.json`.
- Photos and descriptions for each winter item.
- Approval of the page before it goes live.
